# Cat in the Hat: Mac Port

Play the 2003 Xbox game *The Cat in the Hat* on an Apple Silicon Mac, in widescreen and HD, with a modern controller.

This is an unofficial fan project and an early test build. It contains no part of the game. **You need your own copy of the Xbox disc**; the app builds the game from it on your Mac.

## Download

Get `Cat-in-the-Hat-Mac-Port.dmg` from the [latest release](../../releases/latest).

*macOS 13 or later, Apple Silicon (M1 or newer). About 190 MB.*

## What you get

- **Sharp picture.** Up to 4x the Xbox's resolution, in true widescreen.
- **HD textures**, as an optional one-click download inside the app.
- **Better lighting.** Soft shadows, ambient shading, improved water.
- **Any controller.** PlayStation, Xbox and Switch Pro pads, or the keyboard.
- **No waiting.** Company logos skipped, saves kept on your Mac.

## How to set it up

1. Open the `.dmg` and drag the app into Applications.
2. Open the app. If macOS asks whether to open something downloaded from the internet, click Open.
3. Drag your Xbox disc image (`.iso`) onto the app's window and wait about 30 seconds.
4. Click **Download HD** if you want the HD textures (1.4 GB). You can play while it downloads.
5. Press **Play**.

After that, just open the app and press Play.

## Good to know

- It starts a new game with its own saves.
- Settings are in the same window as the Play button, on the VIDEO and GAME tabs.
- The SYSTEM LINK tab does nothing for this game.
- The game's movies play in their original quality.

## Questions

**Where do I get the disc image?** From your own disc. This project can't provide the game and won't link to copies of it.

**The app says my disc isn't recognised.** It currently knows one version of the disc. Open an issue here with what the app shows.

**Is the PC or PlayStation 2 version supported?** No. They are different games.

**Does it work on Intel Macs or Windows?** Not in this release.

**Is this safe to open?** The app is signed and notarized by Apple, so macOS opens it normally.

**Something looks wrong in the game.** Press F11 while it's on screen, then open an issue and attach the files it saves.
